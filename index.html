03 / THE HONEST SMALL PRINT

Almost a journey.
Not yet a reservation.

What the labels mean

Selectable
The exact date and two-person party appeared in a seller flow. Inventory is not reserved.
Referral
A fare appeared before airline checkout. Recheck the final total and conditions.
Search
A schedule and price appeared in search, but the final seller flow was not verified.
Target / pending
A proposed timing or unresolved item, never a ticket or confirmed transfer.

Before any payment

  • Reprice flights, rooms and rail together, and confirm the train runs end to end without a replacement coach.
  • Confirm Daintree seats and every exact hotel pickup. Reserve local taxis and Longreach airport transport.
  • Obtain late-entry arrangements in writing where applicable.
  • Check entry permissions, passport validity, luggage limits, cancellation terms and insurance.
  • Swimming is conditional: follow patrols, stinger advice, closures and the guide. Viewing is always the safer fallback.
